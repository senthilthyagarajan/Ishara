    How to play

    1. Split into teams. On your turn, one player looks at the movie — nobody else.
    2. Start the timer and act it out. No talking, lip-syncing or pointing at things that spell it.
    3. Your team shouts guesses. Tap Guessed it! when they get the full title.
    4. Don't know the movie? Pass before you start the timer.
    5. Easy scores 1, medium 2, hard 3. Most points after all turns wins.

    Classic signals

    • 🎥Crank an old camera — it's a movie
    • 🙏Namaste / hands on heart — Hindi film
    • 🎩Tip a hat — English film
    • ✋Hold up fingers — number of words
    • ☝️Fingers on forearm — which word / syllables
    • 👂Tug your ear — "sounds like"
    • 🤏Pinch fingers — small word (a, the, ka…)
    • 🙌Sweep arms — the whole title at once
