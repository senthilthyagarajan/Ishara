          How to play

          1. Split into teams. On your turn, one player looks at the movie on screen — nobody else.
          2. Start the timer and act it out without speaking, lip-syncing or pointing at things that spell the answer.
          3. Your team shouts guesses. Tap Guessed it! the moment they get the full title.
          4. You can pass before starting the timer if you don't know the movie.
          5. Easy movies score 1, medium 2, hard 3. Most points after all turns wins.

          Classic signals

          • 🎥Crank an old camera — it's a movie
          • 🙏Namaste / hands on heart — Hindi film
          • 🎩Tip a hat / wave like royalty — English film
          • ✋Hold up fingers — number of words
          • ☝️Fingers on forearm — which word / syllables
          • 👂Tug your ear — "sounds like"
          • 🤏Pinch fingers — small word (a, the, of, ka…)
          • 🙌Sweep arms — the whole title at once
